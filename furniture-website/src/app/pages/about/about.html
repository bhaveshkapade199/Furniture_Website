📖 Our Story

Who We Are

Bhavesh Furniture was founded in 2010 by Bhavesh Patel in Ahmedabad, Gujarat with a simple vision — to provide every Indian family with beautiful, durable furniture at honest prices.

Starting with a small workshop and a handful of skilled craftsmen, we have grown into one of the most trusted furniture brands in Gujarat, serving over 5000 happy customers across India.

Our furniture is crafted using premium quality wood (Sheesham, Teak, and Pine), high-grade fabrics, and Italian hardware to ensure durability and elegance.
